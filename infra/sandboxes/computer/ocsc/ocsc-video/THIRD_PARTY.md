# Third-party code in ocsc-video

## Remotion Templates by RVE (MIT)

`src/templates/*.tsx` (and `src/templates/kit.tsx`, in part) are adapted
from Remotion Templates by RVE, https://github.com/reactvideoeditor/remotion-templates
(https://www.reactvideoeditor.com/remotion-templates), which its README states is MIT licensed.
Changes: OCSC colours and copy, props for every word, number and image, frame-driven animation,
and a shared scaled stage.

## Remotion

The engine renders with Remotion (https://remotion.dev), which has its own licence: free for
individuals and companies of up to three people; larger companies need a company licence
(https://www.remotion.pro/license).
